    Better research starts with a better description.

    Founders naturally explain what an invention does. Patent records describe how inventions work. Patrick asks the questions needed to turn a vague idea into a structured technical description before searching.

    Founder says

    “I built an AI-powered recycling bin.”

    Patrick asks

    • How is the material identified?
    • What sensors are involved?
    • How is waste physically redirected?
    • Where does inference happen?

    Patrick searches

    • vision-based material classification
    • electromechanical sorting systems
    • sensor-controlled waste routing
    • automated refuse classification

    From idea to evidence-backed research brief.

    What Patrick does today, in the order it does it.

    1. 01

      Understand the invention

      Patrick interviews the founder and extracts the mechanisms that actually matter.

    2. 02

      Build the search plan

      Patrick creates technical features, search concepts, and queries that the founder can review.

    3. 03

      Investigate the evidence

      Patrick retrieves relevant patent records and identifies exact passages connected to the invention.

    4. 04

      Prepare the handoff

      Patrick produces a structured brief with comparisons, sources, alternatives, and questions for professional review.

    Not a list of links. A research dossier.

    Every run ends in a document you can hand to a professional, exportable as Markdown, LaTeX, or a typeset PDF.

    Patents are the first layer.
